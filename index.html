    HOW BOT SKILL RATING WORKS

    Every decision is graded. Your choice is compared with the best option, given the hands your opponents likely hold, and labelled like a chess move: Brilliant, Best, Excellent, Good, Inaccuracy, Mistake or Blunder.

    Each match scores you against every bot: 60% finishing place, 40% decision accuracy.

    Glicko-2 updates the ratings. Beating higher-rated bots is worth more. RD is the rating uncertainty, calculated behind the scenes.

    Bot games never change Ranked Elo. Leaving a match early leaves it unrated.

    SUPPORT

    REPORT A BUG

    Found something that isn't working? Tell us what happened so we can improve RIVL. Don't include your private sign-in code or other sensitive information.

    Reports are anonymous. We only receive what you type, the category and your browser type, never your email, account or IP address.

    CONTACT US

    For account help, privacy requests, appeals or anything else, email support@rivlpoker.com.

    PLAY MONEY ONLY · 18+

    RIVL is free poker for fun and practice. There is no real-money gambling. Chips, ratings and leagues have no cash value and can't be bought, sold, won as prizes or exchanged for anything. You must be 18 or older to play.

    If gambling is causing problems for you or someone you know, free confidential help is available: call or text 1-800-GAMBLER (US), call 0808 8020 133 (UK) or visit gamblingtherapy.org.

    FAIR PLAY & COMMUNITY RULES

    Ranked matches affect your online Elo; casual and bot games do not. No collusion, outside help during Ranked, multiple accounts or bug exploits. Keep names, chat and photos free of hate, harassment, sexual content, spam and other people's private information.

    Report a photo with REPORT PHOTO on the player's seat. To report chat or a name, email us the room code, the player's table name and roughly when it happened. Breaking the rules can lead to removed content, reset ratings or a closed account.

    YOUR ACCOUNT & DATA

    To see, correct or delete your account, or remove your photo, email support@rivlpoker.com with your table name. We check the account is yours before making changes. Table chat is deleted when its room closes.
