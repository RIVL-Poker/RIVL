Ideas sky

Each idea is a glyph in the sky: one arc on its ring per feature it builds on, and a centre mark for effort (dot small, triangle medium, square large). Sweep your cursor across the ring and the glyphs swell and name themselves. None of these is built or in a pull request. Each says what it would build on and why it suits RIVL. Bigger bets ride a second orbit further out, so the first ring keeps its places.

    Content creation

    Its own solar system, joined to the RIVL galaxy by one line only. Everything content needs from the game gathers at the content creation star first, and that star's line is the only way in. Gold pieces are built already; hologram bubbles are ideas.
