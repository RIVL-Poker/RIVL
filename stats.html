How this is counted

  • Online means the page is open and on screen. It sends a heartbeat every minute; a closed or hidden page drops off at once, one that loses signal after 5 minutes.
  • A player is one account, on any number of devices, or one guest browser. Refreshing or opening a second tab is still one player. Crawlers and automated browsers are not counted.
  • Played online and seated at online tables come from the game server dealing a hand to that seat, so they can't be inflated by the page. Played online counts signed-in players (guests at casual tables are in the seated and hands numbers). Bots are counted apart and never as players.
  • Guests are counted from the day this went live. Before that, the history holds signed-in players only, from their accounts, Ranked games, freerolls and puzzle answers.
  • Days are UTC. Per-player daily records are kept about 13 months; daily totals are kept after that.
